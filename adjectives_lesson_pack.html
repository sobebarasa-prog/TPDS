The Royal Order

When two or more adjectives appear before a noun, English requires them to follow this fixed sequence:

1. Opinion → 2. Size → 3. Age → 4. Shape → 5. Colour → 6. Origin → 7. Material → 8. Purpose
Mnemonic: "Oscar Saw A Silly Cat Often Making Pasta" → Opinion · Size · Age · Shape · Colour · Origin · Material · Purpose
Full example: "a beautiful little old round green French silver jewellery box"
